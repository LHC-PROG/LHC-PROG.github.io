# 共享艺术素材

`orbit-art.webp` 是通过内置 imagegen 工具原创生成的透明背景艺术图像，仅转换格式以优化加载，未裁切或重绘。个人主页与 GitHub Profile 使用同一个文件；Profile 的 SVG 横幅和 MiniCamp 卡片将其嵌入，避免依赖远程图片服务。

生成提示词：

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: original abstract 3D art cutout for an elegant computer science research portfolio and GitHub profile banner. Create one stunning sculptural composition of two intertwining fluid, folded glass-and-brushed-metal ribbons, like a floating continuous orbital loop or folded silk Möbius strip, with a few fine architectural filaments. Silvery pearlescent material, transparent ice blue glass edges, subtle cobalt refractions, beautifully restrained teal-to-lavender iridescence. Gallery quality product photography with subtle studio reflections, nuanced realistic surface texture and sophisticated light scattering. Clean sculptural silhouette with generous transparent negative space around it; the artwork occupies the right two thirds of a wide landscape composition and the left third is mostly transparent so website text can be overlaid. True transparent background. No backdrop rectangle, no text, no letters, no labels, no logos, no watermark, no stars or starburst icons, no tech dashboard, no busy particles, no excessive rainbow neon. The silhouette and composition must read beautifully on both very pale blue-white and deep navy backgrounds. Wide landscape, approximately 2:1 aspect ratio.
